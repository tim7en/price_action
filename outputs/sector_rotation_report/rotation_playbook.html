Reserve Sleeve

Drawdown Deployment Rules

This is separate from the rotating sector basket. The reserve sleeve is a cash buffer that only becomes active when SPY is already in drawdown on the signal date, then stays in SPY until SPY gets back to a fresh high.

Bias Controls

Why This Is Not Forward Looking

The live page uses the same no-leak controls as the benchmark report.

  • Features are lagged by 1 bar before training and prediction.
  • The signal executes one bar after it is observed and then holds for 5 bars.
  • The model used a five-year expanding train and one-year validation with purge and embargo controls already baked into the report engine.
  • The current rotation instruction comes from the latest complete holdout signal, not from future returns or revised macro labels.
