Method & caveats

  • Causal: z-scores are trailing 120m; the oscillator and trend use trailing windows; the GMM is fit through today only for today's label.
  • Context tables are in-sample: quadrant forward returns and regime sensitivity describe history, they are not validated timing rules.
  • Sector indices are point-in-time cap-weighted but survivorship-biased; fundamentals are a current snapshot (no history).
  • The verdict maps to the de-risk ladder only — risk-on ⇒ 1× at most.
