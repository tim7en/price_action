Momentum Oscillator Study

A bounded momentum oscillator — tanh of the average causal z-score of 3/6/12-month returns, oscillating −1…+1 — evaluated on the survivorship-free Wilshire index, 2002-03 → 2026-07. Pre-registered exposure maps, one-month lag, full carry costs. The window includes 2008, which the walk-forward regime test's window did not.

1 · The oscillator

Positive = medium-term momentum above its own history; ±0.75 marks the "deep" zones (dotted rules). It saturates rather than clips, so extreme readings stay comparable across eras.

Method & caveats

  • Causal by construction: rolling z-scores (120m window), tanh squash, no fitted parameters, no thresholds chosen after seeing results.
  • Monthly, one asset. ~23 years ≈ 2–3 full cycles; treat zone estimates (especially the thin extreme zones) as noisy.
  • The trend gate is redundant: months with a positive oscillator sit above the 10-month MA in all but a handful of cases, so "trend-gated" and "long-only" are nearly the same book — the oscillator's positive side is the trend filter, expressed continuously.
  • Relation to the de-risk rule: the oscillator is a momentum signal (continuation), not a stress gauge — it complements, not replaces, the health governor. Where it goes short (osc < 0), remember the long/short book's verdict below before trusting shorts.
